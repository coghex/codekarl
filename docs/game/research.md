# Codekarl vision: Research and technology

Part of [the vision](../vision.md), moved verbatim from it on 2026-10-21. Owner decisions are marked **Firm** with their date; everything else is **Open** or a **Proposal**, as there.

## Research and technology (owner, 2026-10-18 14:45Z, 14:57Z, 15:02Z, 15:08Z and 15:18Z)

Answers to ck-guide's three questions of 14:35Z (at 14:45Z), its three of 14:45Z (at 14:57Z), its two of 14:58Z (at 15:02Z), its two of
15:03Z (at 15:08Z) and its two of 15:08Z (at 15:18Z). At 14:45Z the owner opened with: "research is something that i dont like about
victoria or eu5. if i was going to do it, i would" have the following.

- **Firm (the owner's desired model). A research tree, and a panel of all available research.** Each research "card" in the panel
  represents something that can be researched, and there should be many available at any given time.
- **Firm. Each research has a time cost and a resource cost.**
- **Firm. Pops and the player can start research.**
  - *Owner's examples:* an especially rich merchant may take up a research task if it is related to their business, or a noble may take a
    military research task.
  - The player can also pick one.
- **Firm. Research happens in a specific location.** If a pop started it, it is at that pop's location; if the player did, it is in the
  tag's capital.
- **Firm. No limit on simultaneous research.** There are only two limitations: the costs of research, and the ahead-of-time penalty.
  - **Firm (owner, 14:57Z). One sponsor per research task, for now.** Only one pop or the player at a time is assigned to a given research
    task in a tag. Several sponsors on the same research is "not now, maybe we do that later". Different research can still run at the same
    time without limit.
- **Firm. Ahead-of-time penalty.** All research comes with the date it was invented in real life, and a scaled penalty applies, so that the
  player cannot research something 100 years in the future but would have to wait. Early research is viable, but its cost must be weighed
  against the immediate benefit. The owner restated this in answer to what holds advanced methods back: the penalty "should gate off any
  content that is too far ahead".
  - **Firm (owner, 14:57Z). The penalty affects time only.** By increasing time, it increases the total resources used: the research's
    production method still accepts the same inputs, and simply requires more output. There is no separate resource multiplier.
  - *ck-guide's 14:58Z illustration (not an owner decision):* a project needing 100 progress at 10 a month takes ten months; an
    ahead-of-time requirement of 200 makes it twenty months at the same monthly inputs.
  - No additional literacy, skilled-worker or supply requirement for unlocking research is recorded; those were possibilities raised in
    ck-guide's 14:35Z question, not chosen.
- **Firm (owner, 14:57Z). Time bonuses.** Research gets a time bonus when the technology is held by allies, vassals or overlords, and a
  bonus for neighbouring nations holding it. There is also a bonus for research that is behind in time (past its historical date).
  - The owner floated "perhaps" a sphere-of-influence system like Victoria 2's; it is a possibility, not a decision.
- **Firm. Technology is tag-wide.** All buildings in a country can adopt it. A production method, for example, is adopted only where it is
  deemed profitable; the existing rules still apply: the building's owner (the AI, unless crown-owned) decides, the destination method's
  starting goods are needed, and the change disrupts production (see "Construction, method changes and failing businesses").
- *ck-guide's 14:45Z reading (not an owner decision):* the crown and wealthy pops fund projects for their own reasons, and the panel could
  show each card's normal costs, its historical reference date and the extra cost of researching it now.
- **Firm (owner, 15:02Z). Progress is N out of X.** A research needs a value like N/X, where N is the current research value and X is the
  total needed for it to complete successfully.
  - X is based on the penalties, such as being ahead in time.
  - N is produced at a rate based on live production, so a bonus affects its growth right away.
  - As time goes on, N rises and X drops; when they meet, the research is done.
  - Which of the time bonuses above act on the rate and which on X is not specified.
- **Firm (owner, 15:02Z). Research is like a building that is placed.** It has inputs, outputs, production methods and, importantly,
  owners. Its owner pays for it: the owner's money running out is what pauses it.
- **Firm (owner, 15:02Z). Paused research and resale.** Research pauses when its owner cannot fund it, and other potential researchers may
  pick up the paused research for a cost. An owner that runs out of money needs to be able to sell its "research facility" to another pop
  looking to start some research in that category.
  - This keeps one sponsor per research task: a buyer replaces the owner rather than joining it.
- **Firm (owner, 15:08Z: "yes, that's desireable"). Paused research can still complete.** If X falls to the progress already accumulated,
  the research completes without further production or funding; in ck-guide's 15:03Z example, progress stopped at 80 of 100 completes when
  the requirement falls to 75. The owner confirmed this is desirable, so funding an early project and then waiting is an intended
  strategy.
- **Firm (preference, owner 15:08Z: "im not sure", "for now"). Research facilities are one-off buildings,** perhaps with a short
  construction time. When they are done, they are either converted or destroyed, rather than taking on another research project.
  - *Owner's example:* if a new production method for tools is researched, the research facility automatically becomes a tooling workshop,
    or adds to the level of the existing tooling workshop, "and even have it get the new production method change for free".
  - This is a reward for the facility's owner on top of the tag-wide unlock; it does not make other buildings convert for free.
  - *ck-guide's 15:08Z suggestion (not an owner decision):* make each research card state its completion outcome, so the sponsor knows what
    it is investing in.
  - **Firm (owner, 15:18Z). The research facility's owner owns the result.** If there is an existing tooling workshop with that owner, the
    buildings merge. If the existing workshop has a different owner, a new building is created alongside it, giving two tooling workshops
    with different owners.
  - **Firm (owner, 15:18Z). The free method change covers all of the receiving workshop,** not only the new level: "that's the little bonus
    for researching things". It applies only if the research owner also owns the other building.
  - **Open:** when a facility is converted and when destroyed; which workshop receives the result when the owner has several, and how far
    apart buildings can be and still merge; and what happens to the facility's workers and stock.
- **Open:** what a shortage of inputs does to a project; how the tree's prerequisites relate to which cards are available; whether research
  needs staff; the penalty and bonus curves, and how they stack; the reference dates, which need research; how the sale price is set, and
  whether the crown can buy a facility; what progress passes to the buyer; which pops count as interested in a category; what a facility's
  location affects, given that the unlock is tag-wide; what happens when a project is abandoned, or its location is conquered; and what
  completing other kinds of research gives their owners. No specific research content is chosen.
